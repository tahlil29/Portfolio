Case Study 01 · Product

ToBa Tech — AI Agents & Workflow Automation Releases

Problem

US small-business AI automation work (intake, follow-ups, scheduling, and related workflows) needed clearer scoping so engineering, design, and QA could ship without fuzzy handoffs — requests often arrived as ideas, not buildable tickets.

My Role

As PM Intern I owned requirement notes, acceptance criteria, workflow docs, and delivery tracking for these releases. I did not own the full roadmap; I connected product intent with tickets the team could actually build.

Tradeoff

When a request was too broad for one sprint, I pushed for a smaller, testable slice first — so eng and QA had a clear “done,” instead of a vague epic that stalled.

What I Shipped

Sprint-ready feature briefs and acceptance notes for AI agents & workflow automation releases, plus day-to-day coordination across eng, design, and QA — written so a CS-minded intern could catch gaps early.

Outcome

Clearer handoffs and fewer “what did we mean?” loops when moving from idea to shipped automation work for the US market.
