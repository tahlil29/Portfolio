Design intent

Design intent

  1. Daily Challenge becomes the primary action.
  2. Progress remains visible.
  3. Completion receives lightweight reinforcement.
  4. The user gets a clear reason to return.
  5. Social/leaderboard complexity is intentionally deferred.
Product decisions

Product decisions

  • Primary action: Daily Challenge
  • Secondary: Continue Learning
  • Supporting: Browse Activities
  • Progress: Visible after completion
  • Social: Deferred from v1
Validation plan

Validation plan

Run a controlled test comparing the existing experience to the Daily Challenge intervention. Track D1 retention as the primary decision metric, with challenge completion and first-session engagement as supporting signals.

No measured lift is claimed here — this is the proposed evaluation approach.
