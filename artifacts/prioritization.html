Feature Prioritization

Impact vs Effort for Unroad retention, with a RICE-style check so P0 is not just "what sounds exciting."

Impact × Effort

FeatureImpactEffortPriority
Daily ChallengeHighMediumP0
Progress / StreakHighLowP0
Daily CalendarMediumLowP1
RewardsHighMediumP1
LeaderboardMediumMediumP2

RICE-style check

Reach is all new + returning learners. Impact is highest on Daily Challenge because it changes the home-screen job-to-be-done. Confidence is higher on Progress/Streak because it is a known retention pattern and cheaper to ship. Effort keeps Leaderboard in P2: social surfaces create moderation and comparison risk before the core loop is proven.

Decision

Ship Daily Challenge + Progress/Streak together. Calendar and Rewards follow once participation is instrumented. Leaderboard waits until the loop works without social pressure.
