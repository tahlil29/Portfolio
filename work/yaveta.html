Case Study 03 · Yaveta Solutions

Product planning & cross-functional execution

Product Manager Intern · September 2025 – March 2026 — supporting planning, documentation, sprint execution and design alignment across ongoing product initiatives.

Product ExecutionJiraFigmaUser StoriesCross-functional

Context

Yaveta Solutions operates in an early-stage technology environment where product direction, clear requirements and coordinated delivery all need to move in parallel.

Challenge

Keep multiple product initiatives moving with visible execution — from requirements and user stories through development, testing and delivery — without losing alignment between stakeholders, design and engineering.

My role

Product Manager Intern (September 2025 – March 2026). I supported product planning and day-to-day execution alongside the core team — not as a standalone PM owner, but as hands-on support across documentation, coordination and delivery.

What I contributed

  • Supported product planning and execution across ongoing product initiatives, helping translate requirements into actionable tasks and deliverables.
  • Worked with cross-functional teams to coordinate feature requirements, development activities, testing, and product delivery.
  • Assisted in preparing and maintaining product documentation, requirements, user stories, and project updates.
  • Used Jira to track tasks, monitor sprint progress, manage issues, and coordinate day-to-day product execution.
  • Collaborated with UI/UX teams using Figma to review designs and align product requirements with the intended user experience.
  • Supported stakeholder and technical-team communication to clarify requirements and keep product initiatives moving forward.

Key learning

Strong product execution depends on clear requirements, visible sprint health and tight loops between design, engineering and stakeholders — especially when the team is small and moving fast.
