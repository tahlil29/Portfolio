Case Study 01 · ToBa Tech

Building Product Operations in an Early-Stage Startup

Coordinated across product, engineering and QA to improve execution visibility and follow-up as teams and projects grew.

Product OperationsExecutionStakeholder ManagementProcess Improvement

Context

ToBa Tech is an early-stage technology company where multiple teams work simultaneously across product and development initiatives.

Challenge

In an early-stage environment, maintaining visibility across tasks, ownership, communication and execution becomes increasingly important as teams and projects grow.

My Role

I worked across product operations and coordination, supporting day-to-day execution and improving visibility across ongoing work.

Responsibilities

Task & Workflow Management

  • Maintained task tracking and status visibility
  • Worked with Leantime for task management
  • Followed up on ongoing work and dependencies

Product Operations

  • Managed SOD/EOD reporting
  • Maintained timesheet/task workflows
  • Helped improve day-to-day operational visibility

Cross-functional Coordination

  • Coordinated with development, QA, design and other team members
  • Followed up on task progress
  • Helped communicate requirements and updates

Onboarding

  • Supported onboarding and knowledge transfer for interns/team members
  • Helped organize responsibilities and workflows

Product management skills demonstrated

Product Operations ↓ Stakeholder Management ↓ Execution ↓ Process Improvement ↓ Cross-functional Collaboration

Key learning

Working in an early-stage environment taught me that product management is not only about defining features—it also requires strong execution, communication, prioritization and ownership.
